Physical Assumptions & Model Basis ▼

1. Intensity-Envelope Doppler (10 GHz Equivalent)

In optical IM/DD VLC, direct photodetection tracks the optical power intensity envelope rather than optical phase. The 926 Hz Doppler shift at 100 km/h corresponds to a 10 GHz RF subcarrier modulation envelope ($f_d = rac{v}{c} f_{ ext{rf}}$), representing high-mobility vehicular subcarrier dispersion.

2. Lambertian Channel Loss (-76.9 dB Nominal)

Derived for LED semi-angle $\Phi_{1/2}=60^\circ$ ($m=1.0$), active photodiode area $A_{ ext{pd}}=1.0 ext{ cm}^2$, optical filter transmission $T_s=0.70$ (450 nm blue passband), non-imaging CPC concentrator gain $g=3.0$, misalignment angles $\phi= heta=20^\circ$, and link distance $d=25 ext{ m}$.

3. 2D-CNN AI Detection Gain Baseline

The ~1.85 dB SNR advantage is evaluated against classical linear MMSE equalization at a target $ ext{BER}=10^{-3}$ under 10% normalized CSI estimation error and $1.0 imes\sigma_x$ optical DC clipping. The learned GELU non-linear network inverts the LED saturation transfer curve without noise amplification.
